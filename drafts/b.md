OPEN INTEREST / Daily brief, format v2. Tuesday, 24 November 2026.

LEAD: OKXICE opens tokenized securities trading with limits on access.

The venture between OKX and Intercontinental Exchange announced a phased rollout of 113 securities for round-the-clock trading. [https://www.okx.com/learn/okxice-tsv-launch-going-live] Its own notice says the venue lacks SEC approval and customers' self-custodied assets lack SIPC or FDIC protection. [https://www.okx.com/learn/okxice-tsv-launch-going-live]

OKXICE launched on 23 November 2026, Cointelegraph reports, bringing stocks, exchange-traded funds and other exchange-traded products into the OKX app. [https://cointelegraph.com/news/okxice-launch-113-tokenized-assets-objections] OKX's announcement confirms 113 assets for a rolling introduction, rather than saying every market is already available. [https://www.okx.com/learn/okxice-tsv-launch-going-live] The confirmed list includes Fidelity Investments, eToro and Strive among its issuers and asset managers. [https://www.okx.com/learn/okxice-tsv-launch-going-live]

Each token represents a security entitlement backed by one underlying share, according to OKX. [https://www.okx.com/learn/okxice-tsv-launch-going-live] The company says applicable rights include dividends, voting and participation in corporate actions. [https://www.okx.com/learn/okxice-tsv-launch-going-live]

Trading uses automated liquidity pools on Uniswap v4, running on OKX's X Layer network. [https://www.okx.com/learn/okxice-tsv-launch-going-live] Securitize supplies the infrastructure that brings the securities onto the blockchain. [https://www.okx.com/learn/okxice-tsv-launch-going-live] Smart contracts limit both trading and liquidity provision to eligible wallets. [https://www.okx.com/learn/okxice-tsv-launch-going-live]

Access requires a US-based OKX account, identity verification, an activated wallet and completed onboarding. [https://www.okx.com/learn/okxice-tsv-launch-going-live] Customers control their assets and private keys themselves; the venue says it holds neither. [https://www.okx.com/learn/okxice-tsv-launch-going-live]

The notice describes a temporary SEC exemption, but says OKXICE is neither a registered exchange nor a broker-dealer. [https://www.okx.com/learn/okxice-tsv-launch-going-live] Intercontinental Exchange owns the New York Stock Exchange, but OKX explicitly says the NYSE does not operate or oversee this venue. [https://www.okx.com/learn/okxice-tsv-launch-going-live]

The company warns that token prices can diverge from underlying shares outside market hours. [https://www.okx.com/learn/okxice-tsv-launch-going-live] It also says lost wallet keys cannot be recovered and self-custodied assets have neither SIPC nor FDIC protection. [https://www.okx.com/learn/okxice-tsv-launch-going-live]

NEWS:

[NEWS] CFTC staff allow crypto to count toward operating reserves.

Cointelegraph reports that guidance issued on 23 November 2026 lets regulated trading venues and clearinghouses count bitcoin, ether and qualifying payment stablecoins toward financial-resource requirements. [https://cointelegraph.com/news/cftc-staff-say-exchanges-can-count-bitcoin-toward-operating-reserves] The report specifies minimum valuation discounts of 20% for bitcoin and ether and 2% for qualifying stablecoins. [https://cointelegraph.com/news/cftc-staff-say-exchanges-can-count-bitcoin-toward-operating-reserves] It says separate liquidity requirements remain and the advisory expresses staff views rather than a commission-approved rule change. [https://cointelegraph.com/news/cftc-staff-say-exchanges-can-count-bitcoin-toward-operating-reserves]

[NEWS] Sky's co-founder reports a repayment while depositors await withdrawals.

Bankless reports that Rune Christensen said on 23 November 2026 he had repaid 3 million USDS and still owed about 77 million. [https://www.bankless.com/read/news/sky-co-founder-apologizes-for-77m-loan-behind-stusds-freeze] The publication quotes him calling the borrowing “a big mistake” and reports that the roughly $159 million stUSDS pool remained fully borrowed. [https://www.bankless.com/read/news/sky-co-founder-apologizes-for-77m-loan-behind-stusds-freeze] Bankless explains that deposits finance loans against staked SKY and withdrawals require funds that have not been lent. [https://www.bankless.com/read/news/sky-co-founder-apologizes-for-77m-loan-behind-stusds-freeze]

[NEWS] Cross River enters bitcoin-backed consumer lending.

Cross River Bank and LendingUSA launched loans ranging from $10,000 to $250,000 on 23 November 2026, Cointelegraph reports. [https://cointelegraph.com/news/cross-river-bank-bitcoin-backed-lending-250k-loans] Borrowers can obtain 30%, 50% or 70% of their pledged bitcoin's value, depending on the terms. [https://cointelegraph.com/news/cross-river-bank-bitcoin-backed-lending-250k-loans] The report says a qualified third-party custodian holds the collateral, which can be liquidated if borrowers fail to meet collateral requirements. [https://cointelegraph.com/news/cross-river-bank-bitcoin-backed-lending-250k-loans]

TECH:

[TECH] Potuz proposes registering quantum-resistant keys before replacing validators.

In a 9 November 2026 proposal, Ethereum developer Potuz describes a contract where validators would register future quantum-resistant keys. [https://www.potuz.net/posts/pq/] A preliminary fork would record those commitments; a subsequent fork would disable validators using the existing signature scheme. [https://www.potuz.net/posts/pq/] He calls for independent implementations and testing of the signature-verification machinery before deployment, rather than presenting the design as ready to ship. [https://www.potuz.net/posts/pq/]

[TECH] Terence's Sepolia measurements show faster execution with more work per block.

In measurements published on 16 October 2026, Terence reported that median payload execution time fell from 44 to 26 milliseconds across five comparable clients after Glamsterdam. [https://terencechain.com/writing/glamsterdam-scaling-results-ten-days-in] Average gas used per payload, a measure of computational work, increased by 134% over the comparison period. [https://terencechain.com/writing/glamsterdam-scaling-results-ten-days-in] The observations came from one node per execution client on the Sepolia test network, not an Ethereum mainnet deployment. [https://terencechain.com/writing/glamsterdam-scaling-results-ten-days-in]
